## Tokyo
### Ramen & noodles
- **Ramen-ya Shima** (Hatagaya / Nishi-Shinjuku 5-chome) — to try. #1 on rankings. Reservation only, TableCheck, next-day slots open 08:00 JST; ¥390 fee; cash; closed Sat/Sun/holidays.
- **Ginza Hachigo** — to try. One of Tokyo's most acclaimed; TableCheck opens Sat 09:00 for Tue–Sun; closed Dec 29 – Jan 3 (LY).
- **Kikanbo** (Kanda) — to try. Spicy miso; walk-in queue 30–60 min. LY: Dec 30 11–16, closed to Jan 4.
- **Rokurinsha** (Tokyo Station Ramen Street) — to try. Tsukemen; go at 07:30 morning slot.
- **IRUCA Tokyo** (Roppongi) — to try. Moderate queues; closed Mon.
- ✗ Harukiya (Ogikubo), Shunyacchan (Hachioji) — too far unless nearby anyway.
### Tonkatsu
- **Butagumi** (Nishi-Azabu) — to try. Bookable (Toreta / 03-5466-6775); closed Mon; usually closed Dec 31 – Jan 4.
- **Marugo** (Akihabara) — to try. Near B-PUMP Akihabara; cash; closed Mon/Tue.
- **Narikura** — moved to **Minami-Asagaya**, reservation only (omakase.in). Often called Tokyo's best.
### Desserts, tea & sweets
- **Toraya Akasaka** tea room — to try. Open Jan 1–2 (LY), no reservations.
- **Akasaka Aono** — dango; often sold out by afternoon; closed Sun/holidays.
- **Ginza Sembikiya** fruit parlour — closes Dec 31 – Jan 2 (LY).
- **Pudding Lab** (Ginza EXITMELSA) · **ST.SAWAI ORIONS** (Ginza, pudding parfait; closed weekends) · **HIGASHIYA GINZA** · **Sakurai Japanese Tea Experience** (Minami-Aoyama, walkable from Omotesando; closed ~Dec 29 – Jan 3)
- **Sabo Okuno** (Oku-Shibuya) — kakigori; book ahead.
- **Kissa Berie** (near Omotesando) — cream soda; closed over New Year.
### Bigger meals
- **Aman Tokyo — The Lounge** — seasonal afternoon tea (~¥12–15k), palace views.
- **Grand Hyatt** holiday brunch (Dec 31 / Jan 1–3).
- **Nikudarake** (yakiniku) · **Ginza Chikamitsu** (yakiniku, ~¥15k) · **Izumiya** (Kagurazaka smokehouse) · **Sasaya** (Asakusa sukiyaki).
