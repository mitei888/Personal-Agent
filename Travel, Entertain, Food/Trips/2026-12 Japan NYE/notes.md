# Japan New Year 2026–27 — Notes & research

## Key facts
- Dec 30 is the last "normal" day: most restaurants close Dec 31 – Jan 3. Hotels, shrines and a few old sweet shops stay open.
- Most hours below are **last year's** (2025–26) — re-check ~Dec 20 (reminder set).

## Decisions & ruled out
- **Mt Takao** — done Apr 2025, not worth repeating. Jan 1 = Aman tea + palace moat walk instead of a hike.
- **Mt Mitake / Hakone** — ~2 h each way, too far for Jan 1.
- **Kyu-Asakura House** (Daikanyama) — closed Dec 29 – Jan 3. Next trip (spring/autumn).
- **Sakurai tea, Ginza Hachigo, Gajoen Hyakudan Kaidan, Hama-rikyu (until Jan 2), Shinjuku Gyoen, Imperial Palace East Gardens, Nezu** — closed over New Year.
- **B-PUMP** — likely only Akihabara on Dec 30 (LY 11–19); Ogikubo closed Dec 30 – Jan 2. Calendar posted ~Dec 1.
- **Shibuya Sky** Jan 1 = sunrise event only (¥8,200, tickets ~Nov 26–27) — skipped (late NYE).

## Open items
- Flights HKG ⇄ HND (Cathay; land early Dec 30 if climbing; evening Jan 4 return)
- Train tickets: Jan 2 on sale Dec 2 · Jan 4 on sale Dec 4
- Butagumi Dec 30 dinner · Shima Dec 31 (book Dec 30 08:00 JST) · Kusatsu dinners via Kei
- Visit Japan Web for both, ~1 week before
