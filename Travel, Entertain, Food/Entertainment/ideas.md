## Tokyo
### Gardens, tea & places to sit
- **Hotel New Otani Japanese Garden** (Akasaka) — free, 06:00–22:00, open over New Year; light-ups (NYE countdown, Jan "snow" version).
- **Hama-rikyu Garden** — Nakajima teahouse on the pond (matcha + sweet). Closed Dec 29 – Jan 1; reopens Jan 2 09:00.
- **Happo-en** (Shirokanedai) — garden + Muan teahouse (Fri–Mon 11–15).
- **Kyu-Asakura House** (Daikanyama) — 1919 house + garden, ¥100; closed Mondays and Dec 29 – Jan 3. Best spring/autumn.
- **Meiji Jingu** + inner garden (Jan 1 08:00–16:30) — go early.
- **Imperial Palace moat loop** — 5 km flat walk, Chidorigafuchi / Kitanomaru; lovely at sunset.
- **Todoroki Valley** (Setagaya) — 1 km ravine walk, reopened Mar 2026.
- ✗ Shinjuku Gyoen, Imperial Palace East Gardens, Nezu Museum garden — closed over New Year.
### Views
- **Aman Tokyo The Lounge** (33F) · **Andaz Rooftop Bar** (52F, Toranomon) · **Shibuya Sky** (timed tickets; Jan 1 sunrise event only) · **Azabudai Hills** rooftop garden.
### Active
- **B-PUMP Tokyo** (Akihabara) / **B-PUMP Ogikubo** — bouldering; monthly calendar on Instagram; New Year closures.
- Hikes: ✅ **Mt Takao** (Apr 2025 — not worth going back) · **Mt Mitake** Rock Garden (~2 h each way) · **Kamakura** Tenen trail (~1 h 15) · **Hakone** Old Tōkaidō road (~2 h).
- ✅ teamLab, ✅ street go-karting — done, don't repeat.

## Kusatsu

- **Snowshoe**: Hyōkoku course (2 km return, ~2 h) and Yazawagawa course at Kusatsu ski area — ask the hotel about tours/rental.
